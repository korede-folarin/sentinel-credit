SentinelCredit.

A closed-loop ML platform for BNPL credit risk integrating synthetic data generation, model governance, explainability, drift monitoring, and audit evidence. This page runs the platform's governed champion model, in your browser.

Demonstration on synthetic data. Do not enter real personal or financial details. The model runs entirely in this browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere, stored or logged.

                  How this is built
                  
    
                  
      
                  A governed champion
                  
        
                  Calibrated XGBoost, 13 features, selected and promoted through a champion /
          challenger workflow with an automated evidence gate. One feature was removed because it
          leaked the outcome, a decision taken on validity even though it cost a little Gini.
                  
      
                  Verified, not assumed
                  
        
                  The browser model was converted to ONNX and checked against the Python
          champion before publishing: zero decisions changed across 41,604 customers.
                  
        Re-run the check in your browser →
                  
      
                  Honest about scope
                  
        
                  A customer-level portfolio-risk model, not a validated application-time
          underwriting model. It returns a probability and a review recommendation, never an
          invented credit score or limit.
                  
      
                  Every decision on record
                  
        
                  Each consequential choice, including the mistakes found and fixed, is in the
          Engineering Decision Log.

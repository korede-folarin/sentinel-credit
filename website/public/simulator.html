Where should the line be drawn?

A model produces a probability; the business decides the cutoff. Move it and watch the trade-off: how many applications go to manual review, how many defaults are caught, and who bears the errors. Results are the champion's real held-out test outcomes, aggregated into score bands.

What this shows

This is why the cutoff is treated as policy, not a model setting: the trade-off between workload, losses and who carries the errors needs an owner. Segment-specific thresholds were considered and deliberately not built without that sign-off. The configured cutoff assumes a 15% review capacity, which is still a placeholder.
